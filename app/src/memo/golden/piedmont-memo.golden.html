Executive Summary Commentary

Piedmont Precision Components seeks a $5.0M equipment term loan to fund three Mazak INTEGREX multi-tasking CNC machines, alongside a $2.5M increase & renewal of its existing $5.0M working-capital revolver (to $7.5M). The borrower is a 26-year aerospace/defense precision machinist (AS9100D / ISO 13485) with a diversified blue-chip customer base and a multi-year contracted backlog. FY2025 revenue reached $64.5M (+7.5% YoY), but adjusted EBITDA margin softened to ~7.9% ($5.1M) on elevated labor and new-program qualification costs, lifting leverage to ~3.9x (~4.9x pro forma). Liquidity ($8.2M) and DSC (1.42x) remain inside covenant, and the unlimited guaranty of principal Margaret Holloway provides additional support. Recommended for approval at a 5 — Pass/Watch risk rating, with close monitoring of the margin trend.

General Summary

Founded in 1998 and headquartered in Greensboro, NC, Piedmont Precision Components is a privately held precision-machining manufacturer producing high-tolerance components for aerospace, defense, and medical-device end markets. The company operates a ~110,000 sq ft facility with 182 employees and holds AS9100D and ISO 13485 certifications. Ownership rests with founder-CEO Margaret Holloway, who provides a personal guaranty.

Current Performance

FY2025 revenue of $64.5M grew ~7.5% year-over-year, but profitability softened: gross margin compressed to ~21.6% and adjusted EBITDA margin declined to ~7.9% ($5.1M, from ~9.7% in FY2024) on elevated direct labor, training, and first-article qualification costs ahead of full-rate production. Total funded debt of $20.2M (pro forma $25.2M including the $5.0M equipment loan) puts leverage at ~3.9x EBITDA (~4.9x pro forma) — elevated for the rating but supported by tangible collateral and a contracted backlog. Management projects EBITDA-margin recovery in FY2026 as the new program reaches full rate and qualification spend rolls off; the margin trajectory is the key monitoring item.

Recommendation. Approve the $5.0M equipment term loan and the $2.5M increase & renewal of the working-capital revolver (to $7.5M) at a 5 — Pass/Watch risk rating.
