# DESIGN 0.9.32: the ghost register (the Spreading room's first beats, made alive)

Founder, 2026-09-17, after 0.9.31 live: "the spreading workroom still says only reading... not really
guiding and interactive i need to make even the first iterations more cinematic". Screenshot evidence
`knowledge/proofs/0931/2-reading.png`: honest, flat; a static pre-read table (READ AS / STATEMENTS /
PERIODS), a receipt block, one sentence.

## Concept

The room already reads the file's own text before Boom (statement type, periods, units, line items and
figures). That pre-read becomes THE GHOST REGISTER: it types in at the memo beat as a register in faint
ink, marked "from the file, not yet mapped by Boom". Confirm is a real beat (one banker sentence, ink
pill, receipts typing in as the ladder runs). Reading is watching: the ghost stands with the filling ">"
per file and Boom's own state word, a guided rail (what Boom is doing in its four states, what happens
next, real doors to take meanwhile, the leave sentence), and pins on rows the banker wants to watch.
Arrival is the payoff: Boom's mapped lines light the ghost rows in place, account-code chips settle, a
derived reconciliation line ("Boom agreed on 15 of 17 lines; 2 read differently") shows where Boom read a
line differently from the file text, the brief types above, then the register's controls fade in.

## Doctrine

Nothing on the glass claims a state Boom does not report. The ghost is the file's text, always labelled
as such; the register is Boom's; the reconciliation is derived from the two and never invented. Facts
once. Two doors. No em dashes. Reduced motion reaches the same facts.
