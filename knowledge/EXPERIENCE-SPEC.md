## 1. What we are building

### 1.1 The thesis: conclude, do not display

This is a **commercial credit cockpit**. Its spine is the credit lifecycle, originate then maintain and monitor, and it is a verdict, not a viewer. It opens a $12.5M manufacturing borrower and says, in one banker sentence, whether the relationship is healthy, how big it is, what the one thing to worry about is, and the single best move to make right now. It does not hand the banker a grid of `LLC_BI__TCE__c`, `LLC_BI__Risk_Rating__c`, and an empty Deposits tab and leave them to do the reading.

Every element on screen must earn its place against one rule: if you would render a table, render the **conclusion the table implies** plus the **drill to the detail** for anyone who wants to audit it. The table is the thing you drill *to*, never the thing you are handed and asked to interpret.

The cockpit is the relationship-level surface that spans all six blueprint stages. The deal-level credit memo (`credit-memo-reinvented`, BUILT) is the deep dive on stages 03 to 04. Both sit on one nCino Product Package spine, with Boom as the shared spreading system of record. Commercial credit is the center: KYC, collateral monitoring, and covenant monitoring are first-class through-lines; deposits/treasury wallet, portfolio, and profitability are supporting relationship context, not the organizing axis.

### 1.2 The useful test

Every element on the cockpit must pass three checks:

1. **It answers a question the persona walked in with.** Not a metric they might find interesting. The actual thing in their head when they opened the relationship, at the lifecycle stage it sits in.
2. **It surfaces what changed or what needs attention.** A snapshot that does not flag the watch item is a screensaver.
3. **It offers an action or a drill.** Conclusion plus next step, every time. A conclusion with no door is a dead end.

If an element fails any of the three, it is cut or demoted behind a drill.

### 1.3 What it is NOT

- **Not an autonomous decider.** It is decision-support under SR 11-7. It concludes, surfaces, drafts, and routes. A human commits every credit action. The two genuinely mutating credit verbs (advance the nCino stage, approve the package) are not cockpit buttons; they fire only on an explicit human committee action.
- **Not a place where regulated numbers are guessed.** Exposure roll-ups, covenant grades, collateral coverage, and routing verdicts are deterministic server-side code reading verified fields, mirroring `experience-mcp/boomFinancials.js` where numbers never pass through the model. The LLM ranks and narrates these; it never produces them.
- **Not a sharing bypass.** It runs as the authenticated user, so Salesforce sharing and FLS are the floor on every query. The only floor-bypass is a gated, audited admin god-mode for internal and demo use, and that mode widens visibility, never write authority.
- **Not a generic dashboard reskinned.** If a section reads like a header card with five metric tiles, it has failed the bar and is rebuilt.
- **Not a navigation menu pretending to be a lifecycle.** The six stages are where a relationship actually sits between a first lead and a live, monitored facility, not tabs. An empty stage rendered as a grid is the table the bar forbids; a stage with no real data to conclude from is not built until it has data.

## 2. Design pillars

1. **Concludes from the whole fleet.** The cockpit reads exposure, wallet, covenants, spread, ownership, KYC, and servicing signals across the fleet (nCino, FSC, Boom, Snowflake, AFS) and asserts one opinion per stage and persona. The conclusion is the product; the data is the proof.
2. **Drill-as-provenance.** Every headline is a door. Clicking it descends one provenance level, then the source-of-source, until you hit the system of record. The drill path is the audit trail walked forward by a banker. Lineage (Boom to Snowflake to nCino) is the navigation, not a footnote.
3. **Editable lens, judgment, and narrative; sources locked.** A banker may change how a number is framed, record what they conclude about it, and write the story around it. They may never change the number. The boundary is enforced server-side, not by hiding a button.
4. **Self-learning that ranks, never computes.** The cockpit gets sharper per relationship and across the book by tuning which conclusions surface, in what order, framed how. No learned signal is ever an input to a regulated figure.
5. **Memory: decision ledger plus audit plus recall.** The cockpit remembers why the bank did what it did (the editable decision ledger) and what was done (the append-only audit trail), and warms the next analyst who walks in. Memory informs judgment; it is never the source of a regulated figure.
6. **Policy and regulatory adherence wired into the surface.** SR 11-7 model-risk governance, credit-policy exception testing, KYC/OFAC/PII handling, the exam/QC provenance pack, and the security floor are concrete cockpit behaviors, not a disclaimer footer.
7. **Persona-scoped FLS and partial-view, a named primitive.** Field-level scoping (a row is visible, a field on it is not) and the partial-view marker ("N related entities not visible to you") are one designed control consumed by every section, not a per-section courtesy. Scope is a control, not an afterthought. (Full pillar: §5.7.)
8. **Bank-agnostic via config.** Managed-package namespace plus a per-tenant config map. The same cockpit serves a bank with seeded nCino treasury and a bank running FSC wealth, by config priority, with no code change.
9. **Runs as the user, with a gated admin mode.** Per-user OAuth so the platform enforces sharing and FLS; a permissioned, audited god-mode is the single intentional exception, and it never raises a delegated authority limit.

## 3. The commercial credit lifecycle

This cockpit has a spine, and the spine is the credit lifecycle, not a navigation menu. A commercial banker does not think in "sections"; they think in where a relationship sits between a first lead and a live, monitored facility, and what their job is at that point. The Accenture commercial-lending blueprint names that path as six stages, from origination through maintenance and monitoring. The cockpit organizes itself along those six stages, and the cockpit sections in Section 4 are the **detail under these stages**, not a parallel structure. Read this section first; everything after it is the depth.

The cockpit is the relationship-level surface that spans all six stages. The deal-level credit memo (`credit-memo-reinvented`, BUILT) is the deep dive on stages 03 to 04. Both sit on one nCino Product Package spine, with Boom as the shared spreading system of record and the same Boom to Snowflake to nCino lineage running underneath. Per stage and per persona the cockpit **concludes**, drills to source, and lets the human act inside their lane. It never hands over a stage as a grid of fields.

### 3.0 The spine in one breath

> "The recognized lifecycle of commercial lending, from first lead to a live, monitored facility. Six stages: Prospecting, Sales, Credit Analysis, Approval, Offering & Set-up, Servicing & Monitoring. KYC, collateral, and covenants are the three threads that run the length of it. The cockpit proves on Credit Analysis (03) and Servicing & Monitoring (06) first, because that is where the relationship value and the real Piedmont data both concentrate."

Origination is the left half (01 to 04, win it and underwrite it); maintenance and monitoring is the right half (05 to 06, board it and watch it). Piedmont Precision Components sits at the 03/04 seam, a $12.5M C&I package in credit decisioning with everything underneath it real: exposure, collateral, covenants, ownership, the watch item. It is the worked example for every stage where origination data exists; Timothy Norton Household is the worked example for the wallet-and-household conclusions Piedmont structurally cannot show.

### 3.1 Stage 01 — Prospecting (Salesforce)

**Sub-capabilities.** Lead identification, clustering and scanning, RM assignment, pipeline and opportunity, batch-rating.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Of the names in front of me, which one is worth a call today, and why." Not a pipeline grid. The cockpit ranks the book by attention and whitespace and renewal-due, and for a credit-rich name concludes the cross-sell move before the banker asks. At the book tier it concludes batch context (which cohort is migrating, where the unclaimed wallet sits).

**Cockpit capability / section.** Whitespace and next-best-action (§4.8), the Book / Work-Queue surface (§5.1, the maintenance home screen also used in 06), and the Portfolio roll-up tier (§5.4) for batch context.

**Owning personas.** Relationship Manager / Loan Officer (§6.1, owns this stage); Portfolio / Credit-Risk Executive (§6.7) for the batch-rating and cohort lens.

**Honest data caveat.** Stage 01 has no native prospecting data for Piedmont: there is no lead record, no batch-rating cohort, and the single `Opportunity` is the only pipeline row. The stage is therefore honestly a cross-sell reframe of an existing borrower, driven by the stage-06 renewal clock (the verified 2027 revolver maturity), not a populated prospecting view. This keeps Stage 01 consistent with §3.10's rule (build 03 and 06 first; do not build an empty stage as a grid) and prevents the §3.9 capability table from implying Prospecting is demo-ready on real prospecting data.

**Real-data example.** Piedmont is past prospecting (it is a live package), so the honest example is the **next** relationship: the cockpit concludes "Piedmont is the largest unclaimed operating wallet on a performing Grade 5 name in your book; open against the 2027 revolver renewal." Prospecting for an existing borrower is the cross-sell, not a fresh lead.

### 3.2 Stage 02 — Sales (nCino)

**Sub-capabilities.** Onboarding, application preparation, **pre-scoring and KYC**, mandates.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Who am I really lending to, is the structure clean, and is this party cleared to bank." The KYC through-line opens here. The cockpit concludes the entity-and-ownership shape and states the compliance posture plainly, including when it cannot: if KYC is unpopulated it concludes "unverified, blocks decisioning," never a fabricated clearance.

**Cockpit capability / section.** Entity and ownership graph (§4.2), plus the KYC and compliance read (§4.7) that the entity graph gates to credit/compliance personas. Application exposure cross-links forward to Exposure (§4.3).

**Owning personas.** Relationship Manager (§6.1, front-door KYC/CIP evidence and onboarding); Credit Analyst / Underwriter (§6.3, sees beneficial-ownership detail, KYC-gated).

**Real-data example.** Piedmont concludes "single operating-company borrower, 100% owned and fully guaranteed by one individual principal, Margaret Holloway (PersonAccount, 100% ownership on the `LLC_BI__Connection__c` edge, full-note unlimited guaranty on the `LLC_BI__Legal_Entities__c` edge). Concentration risk is the structure itself, mitigated only by her guaranty." KYC status for Piedmont is **unverified in the sandbox** (`KYC__c` / `Compliance_Check__c` exist; Piedmont population not swept): the cockpit concludes "KYC unverified, this blocks decisioning until cleared," and shows the absence as the conclusion, never green-lights a clearance the data does not support.

### 3.3 Stage 03 — Credit Analysis (Boom · Credit Memo) [BUILT]

**Sub-capabilities.** **Financial spreading**, **collateral estimation**, **KYC and due diligence**, CA preparation.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Do the financials hold up the grade, am I covered if it goes wrong, and is the relationship safe." This is the densest stage and the one where Piedmont is fully real. The cockpit concludes the financial health read (the watch item is born here), the collateral coverage verdict, and the risk/covenant verdict, then seeds the memo. All three through-lines are live at this stage: KYC and DD complete here, collateral is **estimated** here (the first of its three appearances), covenants are tested here against the spread.

**Cockpit capability / section.** Financial spread / Boom snapshot (§4.5), Exposure plus collateral coverage (§4.3), and Risk plus covenant compliance (§4.6). These feed `deal_show_summary` / the deal memo. The deterministic engines (`boom_get_spread`, `boom_get_ratios`, `deal_covenant_grade`) compute every regulated figure server-side; the LLM narrates and ranks, never computes.

**Owning personas.** Credit Analyst / Underwriter (§6.3, owns the spread and the memo); Portfolio Manager (§6.2) and Credit Officer (§6.4) consume the conclusions.

**Real-data example.** Piedmont: "Healthy top line, soft middle. Revenue $64.5M up 7.6% (current Boom actuals), but EBITDA margin compressed to 8.1% (current Boom actual) from ~11% in FY2023 (Boom prior-period read) and leverage is 3.85x (current Boom actual) rising to ~4.8x pro forma (analyst-adjustment basis, not a sourced field). The grade is carried by coverage, not margin." Collateral: "fully covered, ~$10M lendable on 5 `LLC_BI__Loan_Collateral2__c` pledges at 80% advance, 1st lien, against $12.5M committed and >100% of the $4.25M outstanding." Covenants: "all four `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` Compliant; DSC 1.42x against a 1.25x floor is the thinnest cushion in the package (≈14% headroom) and it is thinning because margin is." The watch item the rest of the cockpit keeps pointing back to is **born in this stage**.

### 3.4 Stage 04 — Approval

**Sub-capabilities.** Agreement drafting, pricing and terms, **covenants finalization**, approval decision, signatures.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Does the rating call defend itself, is this within my authority or does it route, and what am I committing to." The covenant through-line crosses from tested (03) to **finalized** (04): the covenant package becomes the binding instrument. The cockpit concludes the risk verdict framed for effective challenge, and concludes the authority state (required approval level vs. acting-officer limit, approve-within-authority vs. route-to-committee), computed server-side against the acting user.

**Cockpit capability / section.** Risk plus covenant compliance (§4.6, framed for the approver), Header plus verdict (§4.1, carrying the delegated-authority context), the Authority / routing model (§5.6), and the approval write path (`ncino_approve_package`). This is one of the two genuinely mutating credit verbs and it fires only on an explicit human committee action; DRAFT-until-committee holds on everything that touches the decision.

**Owning personas.** Credit Officer / Approver (§6.4, holds the decision and the only approve/reject control, separation of duties enforced); Credit Analyst (§6.3, drafts and attests, never decides).

**Real-data example.** Piedmont sits at the **03/04 seam** (Package Stage "Credit Decisioning"). The cockpit concludes for the officer: "Grade 5 Pass/Watch is defensible on coverage; the point to challenge is margin compression eating the DSC cushion; the binding covenant is DSC at 1.42x/1.25x. Approve-within-authority vs. route-to-committee is evaluated against your delegated limit." An analyst add-back that would move the Pass/Watch grade is flagged for officer challenge and blocks same-analyst self-attestation.

### 3.5 Stage 05 — Offering & Set-up (Core banking)

**Sub-capabilities.** Drawdown, account and limit set-up, **covenant tracking**, **collateral valuation**, documentation.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Do the booked terms faithfully match the approved package, and is everything perfected before we fund." Two through-lines hit their middle beat here: collateral moves from estimated (03) to **valued** (`LLC_BI__Collateral_Valuation__c`), and covenant **tracking starts** (the ticklers that 06 will monitor). The cockpit concludes a boarding-readiness verdict, diffing approved vs. booked and flagging every mismatch, and gates the servicing handoff on conditions being satisfied.

**Cockpit capability / section.** Boarding Reconcile (§5.3, approved vs. booked, mismatch-flagged), the collateral-valuation drill under Exposure (§4.3, the `LLC_BI__Collateral_Valuation__c` freshness conclusion), and the servicing-handoff write tools (`create_workpackage`, `reserve_obligation_number`). Ops authors documentation notes only; sourced figures stay read-only and provenance-locked.

**Owning personas.** Loan Operations / Servicing Officer (§6.5, owns this stage, the booking gate). Treasury / Cash-Management Officer (§6.6) **owns the stage-05 booking event as the cross-sell trigger**: the loan-closing / booking moment is the Treasury-owned event (per the MAINTENANCE-AND-MONITORING booking/close/renewal surface), where the operating-wallet play is staged against the newly live facility.

**Real-data example.** Piedmont has not booked (still pre-approval), so the honest conclusion is forward-looking: "On approval, board the 3 facilities ($5M, $5M, $7.5M) against the package rollup; do not naive-sum to $17.5M, the limit/sublimit structure nets to $12.5M TCE. Confirm 1st-lien perfection on the pledged equipment before funding; valuation freshness is a documentation condition." The one number to never get wrong at boarding is the package rollup.

### 3.6 Stage 06 — Servicing & Monitoring (Early Warning)

**Sub-capabilities.** Portfolio monitoring, EWS, **covenant monitoring**, **collateral monitoring**, limit and exposure, collections and recoveries, regulatory reporting.

**What the cockpit concludes here.** "Is this credit holding or sliding, what is due or overdue, and what is about to go wrong." This is the maintenance home, and all three through-lines reach their final, recurring beat: covenant **monitoring** (next-test ticklers, cushion trending toward breach before the test date), collateral **monitoring** (revaluation-due, advance/lendable drift, insurance/UCC/flood expiry, coverage erosion), and KYC **periodic refresh** (sanctions re-screen). The cockpit concludes the downside action (EWS) and ranks it against the upside (whitespace), because a banker does not separate protect-this from grow-this.

**Cockpit capability / section.** Whitespace / next-best-action plus alerts / EWS (§4.8), the Work Queue / Tickler surface (§5.1, the maintenance home screen for PM and Ops), the Portfolio roll-up tier (§5.4, concentration and grade migration), and the Exam / QC provenance pack (§5.5, examiner-ready Boom to Snowflake to nCino lineage, AI-proposed vs. human-attested). Servicing signals come from afs-mcp (`revolver_utilization`, `payment_history`) and corroborate or contradict the Boom trend.

**Owning personas.** Portfolio Manager (§6.2, owns this stage, the book-health conscience); Relationship Manager (§6.1) for renewals and whitespace; Loan Operations (§6.5) for the exception/tickler clock; Portfolio / Credit-Risk Executive (§6.7) for the aggregate deterioration and concentration roll-up.

**Real-data example.** Piedmont: "Watch the margin, not the covenants, yet. All four covenants Compliant, but DSC at 1.42x/1.25x is the thin one (0.17x, ≈14%); margin is the lever under it and it slipped from ~11% to 8.1%. This is a watch, not a breach, schedule the conversation now. Next covenant test is keyed to `LLC_BI__Frequency__c`; the 2027 revolver maturity is the wedge for the wallet play." Upside ranked alongside: "$12.5M of committed credit, $0 operating wallet, the largest unclaimed wallet on a performing name in the book."

### 3.7 The three through-lines

Three threads run the length of the lifecycle, crossing stage boundaries. Fabian named these first-class. Each appears at three points, deepening as it goes; the cockpit treats them as continuous, not as one-stage events.

| Through-line | Stage 02/03 (originate) | Stage 04/05 (set up) | Stage 06 (maintain) | Source of truth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KYC** | Pre-scoring and KYC (02), KYC and due diligence (03), origination clearance | (carried in the package; clears before funding) | Periodic refresh, sanctions re-screen | `KYC__c` / `Compliance_Check__c`; beneficial ownership from `LLC_BI__Connection__c`; OFAC / adverse media. Piedmont: Margaret Holloway 100%; KYC population **unverified**, conclude "blocks decisioning" if empty, never fake clearance. |
| **Collateral** | Estimation (03) | Valuation (05), `LLC_BI__Collateral_Valuation__c` | Monitoring (06): revaluation-due, advance/lendable drift, insurance/UCC/flood expiry, coverage erosion | `LLC_BI__Loan_Collateral2__c` (advance rate, lendable value, lien, abundance-of-caution) + `LLC_BI__Collateral_Valuation__c`. Piedmont: 5 pledges, 80% advance, 1st lien, lendable ~$10M. |
| **Covenant** | Tested against the spread (03) | Finalization (04), tracking starts (05) | Monitoring (06): next-test ticklers, cushion, breach-trending before the test date | `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` (threshold `LLC_BI__Financial_Indicator_Value__c`, actual `LLC_BI__Last_Evaluation_Value__c`, status, `LLC_BI__Frequency__c`) + `LLC_BI__Covenant_Compliance2__c` history; deterministic `deal_covenant_grade`. Piedmont: 4 covenants all Compliant, DSC 1.42x/1.25x thinnest. |

The through-lines are why the cockpit reads as one continuous relationship and not six disconnected screens: the covenant the analyst tests in 03 is the same covenant the officer finalizes in 04, the ops officer begins tracking in 05, and the portfolio manager monitors in 06, against the same `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` record and the same deterministic grade.

### 3.8 The supporting relationship context

Deposits and treasury share-of-wallet, the entity-and-ownership graph beyond its KYC role, portfolio roll-up, and profitability ride the relationship across all six stages but are **not the spine**. They are the growth lens for the RM and the Treasury officer, secondary to the credit through-lines. The $0-wallet next-best-action (§4.4) is the clearest of these: it is the headline move on Piedmont, but it is relationship-development context layered on a credit spine, not the organizing axis. Timothy Norton Household ($910,800, populated wallet and household) is where this supporting context becomes the lead, and it does so precisely because that relationship has no credit lifecycle to organize around.

## 4. The cockpit sections

These are the eight section contracts that are the **depth under the lifecycle stages**. The seven relationship sections from v1 are preserved unchanged in substance; KYC & compliance (§4.7) is elevated to a first-class section (previously distributed across §4.2's gated detail and the policy pillar). Each section is tagged with the blueprint stage(s) it serves.

Eight sections, six relationship read tools (`customer_360`, `relationship_entities`, `relationship_exposure`, `deposits_and_treasury`, `relationship_risk`, `relationship_opportunities`) plus the Boom spread surface and a dedicated KYC/compliance read tool (`relationship_kyc`, committed as the KYC section's tool contract; see also Open Question §13.10). Early drafts enumerated eight relationship sections by splitting whitespace from alerts; the contract fuses those two, because a banker thinks "what do I do about this relationship" from the upside and downside at once.

Each section is one contract: **Question / Conclusion / Evidence and drill path / Actions / Per-persona variation / Data anchors.** The Data anchors block is a provenance appendix: each field entry carries a one-clause "what it concludes or why it is load-bearing." Fields cited inline in the evidence are not re-listed.

---

### 4.1 Relationship header plus verdict (including profitability)

*Blueprint stages: spans all six; the authority context belongs to 04.*

**Question.** "In one breath: is this relationship healthy or not, how big is it, what is the one thing I should worry about, and what is the single best move I can make right now?"

**Conclusion.** The header asserts one banker-grade sentence with the watch item and the next-best-action baked in. For Piedmont:

> "Piedmont Precision Components, Pass/Watch (Grade 5), $12.5M committed / $4.25M drawn across 3 facilities. Holding, but margin is the story: EBITDA compressed to 8.1% from ~11% and the DSC covenant cushion is the thinnest in the package at 1.42x against a 1.25x floor. Biggest move on the table: this is a $12.5M credit with a $0 operating wallet, win the deposits and treasury."

The four things the persona walked in with, in one sentence: rating (Pass/Watch, Grade 5), exposure ($12.5M / $4.25M, trust the package rollup), the single watch item (margin compression, surfaced as the *why* behind the thinnest covenant cushion, not a list of all four covenants), and the headline next-best-action (the $12.5M-credit / $0-wallet gap).

The profitability lens is the second clause, and it is honest about absence: for Piedmont, profitability is empty (0 records), and **empty is not zero, it is "unmeasured."** The header asserts "relationship profitability is unmeasured, we earn spread on $4.25M of credit and capture none of the operating economics, because there is no deposit, treasury, or fee relationship to measure." The absence of a profitability record is itself the conclusion, and it points at the same lever as the exposure verdict.

For **Timothy Norton Household** the same header concludes differently, because the data supports it: "$910,800 in deposits across the household, linking Timothy Norton (owner) and Smart Snacks (operating business). Deposit-led relationship; the verdict and next-best-action live on the wallet side." Piedmont proves the credit verdict and the wallet gap; Timothy Norton proves the household and wallet verdict Piedmont structurally cannot.

For the **Credit Officer at stage 04**, the header carries a fifth evidence anchor: the **routing chip** (§5.6). It concludes whether this package is the acting officer's to approve or routes to committee ("$12.5M / Grade 5 exceeds your $10M delegated limit, route to committee"), persona-resolved so it renders as a personal verdict only for the approve-capable persona.

The verdict sentence is generated from deterministic inputs, but the prose lens is editable. The numbers inside it (8.1%, 1.42x, $12.5M, Grade 5) are sourced and locked.

**Evidence and drill path.** Beneath the verdict sits a thin evidence rail, a row of claims each carrying its own drill (conclusion plus where it came from plus click to go deeper). Each drill carries the verdict's framing forward, so the banker lands on an answer, not a fresh table:

Adjustment record (adjusted EBITDA / add-backs and the pro-forma leverage as `{value, basis:'analyst-adjustment', rationale, by, asOf}`; if it feeds `deal_covenant_grade`, the grade is computed on both bases with dual lineage and a grade-boundary move is flagged for officer challenge). Draft-narrative (the financial-analysis memo section). Schedule/trigger (watch margin below a threshold or a new spread period landing).

**Per-persona variation.** Analyst (full, deepest of any persona, line-item depth, owns the spread); PM (full, the trend read, 11% to 8.1%, 3.85x to ~4.8x); Credit Officer (full, ratio actuals plus grade evidence for the rating call); RM (summary, "revenue $64.5M, +7.6%, margin compressing" as context, not the line items); Treasury (summary, "healthy enough to justify pursuing the wallet"); Loan Ops (summary or hidden, not their work); Executive (summary, book-aggregate financial health, drill to a single spread on demand).

**Data anchors (provenance, with conclusions).**
- `boom_get_spread` (`financialStatements[]` to `lineItems` by `accountCode` to `periodValues`) — the line-item source every ratio decomposes to; off-Salesforce, the system of record; carries both the current period (8.1%, 3.85x) and the FY2023 prior period (~11%).

- `Boom_*__c` (SF mirror) — the same figures inside Salesforce for join and lineage.
- `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c.LLC_BI__Linked_Spread_Statement_Record__c` — the tie that lets a covenant actual drill back to its spread period.

### 4.6 Risk plus covenant compliance

*Blueprint stages: 03 Credit Analysis (tested against the spread), 04 Approval (covenants finalization, the verdict for effective challenge), 06 Servicing & Monitoring (covenant monitoring, see §5.2).*

**Question.** "Is this credit holding or sliding? How much room before a covenant trips? Does the rating call still defend itself? And if a number looks wrong, where did it come from?" The persona is not asking to see covenants; they are asking the section to conclude whether the relationship is safe and whether the grade is honest.

**Conclusion.** One banker sentence, then the defense. For Piedmont:

> "Grade 5, Pass/Watch, holding, but the watch is real: DSC at 1.42x against a 1.25x floor is the thinnest cushion in the package, and it is thinning because EBITDA margin compressed from ~11% (FY2023) to 8.1% on $64.5M revenue. All four covenants are Compliant today; the question is the next two quarters, not this one."

**The cushion convention is fixed and applied everywhere: headroom relative to the threshold floor.** DSC cushion = (1.42 − 1.25) / 1.25 = 13.6%, shown as ≈14%. The absolute 0.17x is the primary figure; the percentage is secondary. This same convention is used in the header, the whitespace section, the covenant-monitoring surface, and persona §6.2 (no "~12%" variant).

**Evidence and drill path.** The section face renders **only the binding covenant as a conclusion**: "DSC 1.42x vs 1.25x, 0.17x of headroom (≈14%), the only covenant a soft EBITDA quarter trips; the other three are wide." The other three collapse into a single conclusion with no bare pairs on the face: "the other three covenants are all wide (smallest cushion among them is Debt-to-Worth at 27% headroom), none within a soft-quarter of breach, drill for the threshold/actual grid." **The four-row threshold/actual/status grid lives behind the drill, never on the face.** The "thinnest is computed, the rest are one line" rule actually holds.

The thinnest ranking is **deterministic and computed on normalized cushion (percent of threshold), not raw deltas** (which would mix ratio deltas and dollar deltas): DSC 13.6% < Debt-to-Worth 27% < Liquidity 64% < Fixed-Asset 83%. DSC is thinnest by the normalized math, surfaced as the conclusion.

The grade is `deal_covenant_grade` (threshold times actual, deterministic, never the LLM). Drill the DSC actual to `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` (threshold `LLC_BI__Financial_Indicator_Value__c`, actual `LLC_BI__Last_Evaluation_Value__c`, status `LLC_BI__Last_Evaluation_Status__c`, type `LLC_BI__Covenant_Type__r.Name`); then to the spread period via `LLC_BI__Linked_Spread_Statement_Record__c`; then to the Boom line items (the load-bearing lineage, the actual leaves nCino and enters Boom). Per-period history is `LLC_BI__Covenant_Compliance2__c` ("compliant as of when, approved by whom"). Risk wrapper: `LLC_BI__Relationship_Risk_Review__c`. Legacy/modern guard: read `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` (628 org-wide), never legacy `LLC_BI__Covenant__c` (0 records).

**Actions.** Drill the binding covenant to its definition, to the spread period, to the Boom line items, to the per-period history, and the grade to its `deal_covenant_grade` breakdown. Lens-change as-of period, absolute vs relative cushion, sourced-only vs sourced-plus-adjusted grade basis. Annotate / draft-narrative (the risk narrative; sourced numbers locked). Capture-decision (`record_decision`, the rating-call WHY). Analyst adjustment (an add-back as a first-class record; if it moves a Pass/Watch/Breach grade it is flagged for officer challenge and blocks same-analyst self-attestation). Approve/reject (Credit Officer only, `ncino_approve_package`, gated server-side against Loan Approver permission and the routing verdict of §5.6). Schedule/trigger (a watch flag and a next-evaluation reminder keyed to `LLC_BI__Frequency__c`; subscribe to early-warning when DSC drifts toward the watch band, the covenant-monitoring tickler of §5.2).

**Per-persona variation.** RM (read-only, the one-sentence verdict); PM (full, the four-covenant cushion with trend, may propose, no grade write); Analyst (full, the load-bearing drill to the spread period, files adjustments, no approval); Credit Officer (full, the only approve/reject, separation of duties enforced); Treasury (read-only, the verdict as cross-sell context, FLS-before-aggregation); Executive (full, read-only oversight, aggregated watch/breach counts from the same deterministic grade, never an LLM tally).

**Data anchors (provenance, with conclusions).**
- `LLC_BI__Covenant2__c` — modern, 628 org-wide and populated; legacy `LLC_BI__Covenant__c` = 0 rows, never read it. Threshold/actual/status/frequency/type fields cited inline.
- `LLC_BI__Covenant_Compliance2__c` — per-period history ("compliant as of when, by whom").
- `LLC_BI__Account_Covenant__c` / `LLC_BI__Loan_Covenant__c` — the Account and Loan junctions to Covenant2.
- `LLC_BI__Relationship_Risk_Review__c` — the relationship-grain risk wrapper.
- `LLC_BI__Product_Package__c.LLC_BI__Risk_Rating__c` ("5"), `LLC_BI__Loan__c.LLC_BI__Risk_Grade__c` — deal and loan grades.
- `deal_covenant_grade` (experience-mcp) — the deterministic grade; the LLM never computes it.
- Config knobs: `watchThresholdPct` (the most load-bearing knob, turns Compliant-but-thin into a Watch flag); covenant generation (support both per tenant); `personaResolution` (approval-capable role wins the approve/reject control); FLS-before-aggregation; `sharingModelGrantsHierarchyRollup` (gates the Executive/PM book-level roll-up; when false/unknown, collapses to own book). The next-evaluation date is computed (last evaluation + `LLC_BI__Frequency__c`) or config-mapped, never asserted against an unverified field API name. ### 4.7 KYC & compliance clearance (origination + ongoing CDD refresh)

*Blueprint stages: **02** (Pre-scoring & KYC), **03** (KYC & due diligence), and **06** (periodic refresh / sanctions re-screen). The KYC through-line elevated to a first-class section: it was previously only a clause in the policy pillar (§10.4) and a gated detail under the ownership graph (§4.2). KYC is not a footnote to lending; it is the gate that lets lending happen and the clock that keeps it legal.*

**Read tool.** This section reads through `relationship_kyc` (the committed KYC/compliance read tool, eighth alongside the six relationship read tools and the Boom spread surface; net-new vs folding into `compliance_clearance` is the only open detail, §13.10).

**Question.** "Is this borrower, and everyone behind it, cleared to do business with, and is that clearance current? Specifically: do we know who actually owns and controls this company, have they been screened against sanctions and adverse media, is the file complete enough to decision a credit on, and when does that clearance go stale?" The Analyst and Credit Officer walk in needing a clearance verdict they can rely on before they write or approve a grade. The RM walks in needing only the front-door answer (cleared / not cleared / pending) plus what evidence is still owed, never the screening internals. This section answers both the origination question ("can we lend?") and the maintenance question ("is the clearance we relied on still valid?").

**Conclusion.** One clearance sentence, and it is honest about absence. **The empty-not-pass discipline is absolute here, the same discipline the wallet section applies to the $0 deposits: an unpopulated or unswept KYC object is "unverified," never "clear."** A blank compliance file is the most dangerous false positive in the cockpit, so the section refuses to manufacture clearance.

For Piedmont, the beneficial-ownership half is real and asserts cleanly; the screening half was **not swept** in the verification pass (only deposits/treasury/profitability were confirmed empty), so the section treats clearance as unverified and says exactly that:

> "Beneficial ownership is known and clean: Margaret Holloway holds 100% and is the full-note guarantor, a single identified natural person behind the entire $12.5M credit, no hidden layers, no undisclosed co-owners. But KYC and sanctions screening are **unverified**: `KYC__c` / `Compliance_Check__c` population for the borrower, the guarantor, and the principal was not established by the sandbox sweep. **Clearance is unverified, and unverified blocks credit decisioning.** This is a gate, not a warning: the grade cannot be relied on for a commitment until OFAC, CIP, and CDD evidence is confirmed on file against all three parties."

The two halves are deliberately separated, because they have different truth status and must never be blended into a falsely-reassuring "looks fine":
- **Beneficial ownership (known, real, asserted):** the structure is fully identified. One natural person, 100%, owner = guarantor = control = recourse. The transparency itself is the positive conclusion: there is no opacity to screen *through*. (This is the same fact §4.2 concludes as concentration; here it is read as KYC transparency, the inverse-sided reading of the identical sourced edge.)
- **Screening & due-diligence clearance (unverified, gating):** absence of confirmed evidence is not absence of risk; under BSA/CIP it is itself the finding. The section concludes "blocks decisioning," routes the gap, and never lets a downstream section quietly treat the credit as bankable.

For **Timothy Norton Household**, KYC/Compliance population was also **not swept** in the verification pass (same status as Piedmont), so the section concludes the same way (unverified, confirm or refresh the file) rather than inventing a household clearance; the demo value of Timothy Norton is the wallet, not a faked compliance pass. Clearance is asserted only on real, confirmed rows, for either account.

**The maintenance read (stage 06).** When clearance *is* on file, the section's second job is the staleness clock: "cleared, but the CDD refresh is due / overdue." A KYC record with a refresh-due date in the past concludes "clearance lapsed, re-screen required before the next material action," exactly as a covenant past its test date concludes "tickler due." Origination asks *did we clear?*; servicing asks *is the clearance still alive?*. Same section, two questions, one set of objects.

**Evidence and drill path.** The section face renders **only the clearance verdict and the single most material gap or stalest item**, never the raw screening grid. The four-part evidence backing (CIP/KYC record, sanctions screen, beneficial-ownership graph, adverse media) lives behind the drill.

1. **Beneficial ownership: identified, 100%, single natural person.** The asserted-real anchor. Source: `LLC_BI__Connection__c` (`LLC_BI__Total_Direct_Indirect_Ownership_Percent__c` = 100), grounded in the verified 208-edge Connection graph, **not a re-keyed attestation**, drilled to the guaranty edge in `LLC_BI__Legal_Entities__c` (Borrower_Type Guarantor, Entity Type Individual, full-note). Source-of-source: KYC / beneficial-ownership intake recorded onto the Connection edge at onboarding (stage 02). The drill stops at structure: name, role, ownership %, control, guaranty amount. It **never** descends to SSN/TIN/DOB/personal-address, even where FLS would permit, the PII floor is enforced server-side below the structure layer, not by hiding a field.
2. **KYC / CIP file: confirmed, absent, or unswept (here: unverified → blocks).** Source: `KYC__c` (clearance record, refresh-due date). On an unswept-or-zero state the server reaches the conclusion "no confirmed CIP file on record," and that conclusion is loud, not blank. When populated, the drill shows the clearance status, the CDD risk rating (standard / enhanced), and the next-refresh date that drives the stage-06 tickler.
3. **Sanctions / OFAC / adverse-media screen: confirmed, absent, or unswept (here: unverified → blocks).** Source: `Compliance_Check__c` (the screening result per party). On an unswept-or-zero state: "no confirmed OFAC / sanctions / adverse-media screen on file for borrower, guarantor, or principal." When populated, the drill shows per-party hits-or-clear, the screen date, and the disposition; a true hit is loud and routes to BSA escalation, never auto-cleared by the cockpit.
4. **Scope of screening: all parties, not just the entity.** The section concludes against borrower **and** guarantor **and** principal (Margaret Holloway is all-but-the-entity in one person, so the cockpit must confirm she is screened in every role she occupies). Beneficial owners and guarantors that the running user cannot see are surfaced as a "partial view, N related parties not visible to you" marker (§5.7), never silently dropped, so an incomplete screen is never read as a complete one.

Provenance lock: ownership %, guaranty amount, clearance status, screen disposition, and refresh dates are sourced and read-only; a banker who disputes one disputes it at the source, never in the narrative. The "blocks decisioning" state is a deterministic server-side conclusion (no confirmed qualifying rows → blocked), **never an LLM judgment about risk**, and the model never narrates a clearance the records do not support.

**Actions.**
- **Drill** ownership to `LLC_BI__Connection__c` / `LLC_BI__Legal_Entities__c` (structure only), the KYC verdict to `KYC__c`, the screen to `Compliance_Check__c`, each carrying the clearance framing.
- **Lens-change** between origination clearance (stage 02/03: "can we lend?") and ongoing-CDD (stage 06: "is clearance current?"), and between per-party views (borrower / guarantor / principal). A presentation choice; edits no sourced figure.
- **Annotate** a due-diligence note (free-text, fenced as untrusted data); never edits a clearance status.
- **Capture-decision** (`record_decision`, proposed-not-committed): record a CDD judgment such as "accept standard-CDD given single identified owner and transparent structure" or "require enhanced due diligence pending source-of-wealth," logged with server-set actor and timestamp the agent cannot override. The cockpit never *makes* the BSA call; it records the human's.
- **Route the gap, never clear it:** stage a task to obtain or confirm the missing CIP/OFAC evidence, or route to the BSA/Compliance queue. The cockpit can flag, schedule, and route a screening; it can never assert a clearance, lift a block, or file a SAR. These are human/compliance-system actions outside the cockpit's authority by design.
- **Schedule / trigger** the stage-06 refresh: set a CDD re-screen tickler keyed to the `KYC__c` refresh-due date (same tickler discipline as a covenant next-test date, §5.1), and subscribe to a sanctions-list-update re-screen trigger. Decision-support timing only; the human commits the re-screen.
- **Draft-narrative:** seed the memo's KYC / due-diligence paragraph from the clearance conclusion (or, here, the "unverified, blocks" finding), with the beneficial-ownership structure attached, handed to `ncino_deal_prep`.

**Per-persona variation.** Same spine; what changes is depth of screening detail and write capability, gated server-side, **not** seven code paths. The governing rule: **screening internals are credit/compliance-only; producers see the front-door verdict, never the file.**

- **RM / Loan Officer** (front-door only). Sees the clearance **verdict** (cleared / unverified / pending) and **what evidence is still owed**, the front-door KYC/CIP evidence the RM owns at origination. Does **not** see OFAC dispositions, adverse-media detail, or CDD risk internals. Can stage the "obtain missing evidence" task; read-only on every sourced figure. For Piedmont the RM reads "ownership known and clean (Holloway 100%); compliance screen unverified, owed before commitment."
- **Credit Analyst / Underwriter** (full, feeds the memo). Sees the full screening file and beneficial-ownership detail (KYC-gated), drafts the due-diligence narrative, and **must not write or rely on a grade while clearance is unverified**, the section's block is a hard input to the analyst's workflow, surfaced beside the spread. Records CDD judgments as decisions; never disposes a screen.
- **Credit Officer / Approver** (full, the gate enforcer). Sees the full file for effective challenge; **the unverified-KYC block is a precondition the officer cannot approve through**, `ncino_approve_package` against a borrower with no confirmed CIP/OFAC file is the canonical "decision-support refuses to enable an unsafe action" case. The officer can record the rationale and route, but the block is server-enforced, not advisory.
- **Treasury / Cash-Management Officer** (front-door + onboarding lens). Treasury collects KYC/CIP/CDD and beneficial ownership at **account opening** and screens OFAC/PEP **before services go live**, so this persona sees the clearance verdict and the account-opening screening gate (an open over a CIP/OFAC hit is forbidden), but not the credit-side CDD internals. The "$0 wallet" cross-sell cannot be actioned into an account opening until the screen clears, the KYC gate sits in front of the wallet play (§4.4).
- **Portfolio Manager** (full read, refresh lens). Reads clearance as a **staleness / refresh-due** signal across the book (which relationships have lapsed CDD), feeding the Work-Queue tickler (§5.1); may propose a refresh, no clearance write.
- **Loan Operations / Servicing Officer** (boarding + servicing-screen lens). Screens new parties at **boarding** and monitors them in servicing; reads the clearance state as a fund/no-fund precondition and a re-screen-on-new-party trigger; authors documentation notes only.
- **Portfolio / Credit-Risk Executive** (book-aggregate, read-only). Sees aggregate clearance health ("N relationships with unverified or lapsed KYC," exam-readiness on screening records), drills to the name; no per-relationship clearance write.

**PII / GLBA fence (applies to every persona).** The beneficial-ownership drill returns name, role, ownership %, entity type, and guaranty amount only, never SSN / TIN / DOB / personal address, even when FLS would permit, because the cockpit's job is identification of control, not exposure of consumer PII. Margaret Holloway and the Timothy Norton principals are PersonAccounts carrying consumer PII; the run-as-user floor means a banker without sharing on the person record does not see the person at all, and a commercial-only persona never traverses into an individual's personal accounts. Screening *results* (hit / clear, screen date) are visible to credit/compliance personas; the *underlying personal identifiers* used to screen are not surfaced by the cockpit. (Full fence: §5.7.)

**Data anchors (provenance, with conclusions).**
- `KYC__c` — the CIP/CDD clearance record and refresh-due date. **Verified to exist in the sandbox; population for Piedmont was NOT swept (only deposits/treasury/profitability were confirmed empty).** Unswept/empty → the deterministic "no confirmed CIP file, clearance unverified, blocks decisioning" conclusion; confirmed-populated → the clearance status + the stage-06 refresh clock. Clearance is asserted only on real, confirmed rows.
- `Compliance_Check__c` — the OFAC / sanctions / adverse-media screening result, per party. **Verified to exist; population for Piedmont NOT swept.** Unswept/empty → "no confirmed screen on file" (loud); confirmed-populated → per-party hit/clear + disposition; a true hit routes to BSA escalation, never auto-cleared.
- `LLC_BI__Connection__c` (208 edges org-wide) — the **asserted-real** beneficial-ownership anchor: Margaret Holloway 100% (`LLC_BI__Total_Direct_Indirect_Ownership_Percent__c`). The transparency conclusion ("single identified natural person, no hidden layers") rests on this populated edge, not on a KYC attestation that has not been confirmed.
- `LLC_BI__Legal_Entities__c` — the full-note guaranty edge (Borrower vs Guarantor, Entity Type Individual, `LLC_BI__Guaranty_Amount__c`), confirming the screened party is also the recourse party; a distinct edge from ownership, never conflated.
- `FinServ__AccountAccountRelation__c` (FSC, 8 edges) — enriches related-party role semantics where present (household member / business owner) so the screening scope catches every party; enriches, never replaces, Connection.
- Beneficial-ownership PII below the structure layer (`SSN`/`TIN`/`DOB`/personal-address on the PersonAccount) — **deliberately not returned**, the PII fence is the conclusion that they are out of scope, not a field to render.
- Config knobs: `kycObjectConfig` / `complianceObjectConfig` (per-tenant object + status-field mapping, since a bank may use its own CIP object names; the "unverified → blocks" rule travels with the config); `cddRefreshField` (the refresh-due date driving the stage-06 tickler); `screeningScopeRule` (which related parties from `groupingKey` must be screened: borrower + guarantor + principals); `personaResolution` (credit/compliance personas see the file, producers see only the verdict); FLS-before-render on every screening field. The section carries **no LLM-computed regulated figure and no LLM-judged clearance**; clearance is a deterministic function of real, confirmed rows, and absence (or an unswept state) is the most important conclusion it can reach.

### 4.8 Whitespace / next-best-action plus alerts / EWS

*Blueprint stages: 01 Prospecting (the cross-sell move), 06 Servicing & Monitoring (EWS, the maintenance home).*

**Conclusion.** One asserted upside, one asserted downside, ranked, each a sentence and an action.

Upside (Piedmont):
> "Win Piedmont's operating deposits and treasury. We hold $12.5M of committed credit and $4.25M outstanding, and $0 of their operating wallet. This is the largest unclaimed wallet on a performing Grade 5 relationship in your book. > "Watch the margin, not the covenants, yet. All four covenants are Compliant, but DSC is the thin one at 1.42x against a 1.25x trigger, a 0.17x cushion (≈14%). > "The 2027 revolver maturity is the wedge for the wallet play: open the treasury cross-sell now while they still need the renewal; the 2031 equipment term is out of scope."

The conclusion is the sequencing move (renewal timing into cross-sell leverage), not a restatement of maturity dates. No countdown is invented where the data does not support one.

Provenance-locked: lens, ranking dimension, and narrative are editable; sourced numbers are not. Memory may surface "last quarter we flagged margin too" via `recall_decisions`, but memory is never the source of the 8.1% (always re-read from Boom).

**Actions.** Drill any conclusion's evidence in place. Lens-change the ranking dimension (revenue-at-stake, risk-velocity, renewal-clock, fee-income upside), per-persona config. Schedule/trigger: stage a draft opportunity for human confirmation, set a covenant-watch reminder keyed to `LLC_BI__Frequency__c`, or kick the renewal package-prep. Within the SR 11-7 fence, these stage or route a human action; none commits a credit action autonomously, and the DRAFT-until-committee banner persists on anything touching the credit decision.

## 5. Maintenance and monitoring surfaces plus the cross-cutting pillars

This section is the maintenance-and-monitoring half of the cockpit (blueprint stages 05 and 06) plus the two cross-cutting governance pillars (authority/routing at 04, FLS everywhere). It folds in six of the seven role-research gaps as named surfaces and elevates the collateral- and covenant-monitoring beats out of the section contracts. Each surface holds the same contract bar (conclude, drill, act) and the same SR 11-7 fences. Gaps 3 (authority/routing) and 7 (persona-FLS) are the two pillars at the end; gaps 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 are the five surfaces. ### 5.1 Work Queue / Tickler (gap 1) — the maintenance home screen

*Blueprint stages 06 and 05.* The cross-book "what is due or overdue" surface, persona-filtered, the home screen for the Portfolio Manager and Loan Ops and the second screen for the RM. It concludes the **single most urgent item and why**, not a calendar grid. Ranking is **deterministic normalized urgency** (server-side, never the LLM), so a not-yet-due thin-cushion covenant test can outrank a mildly-overdue routine review. The urgency score is a deterministic function of sourced inputs (days-to-test, cushion-percent, regulatory-clock); learning may only re-weight *across* tickler classes, never compute a per-item urgency from the regulated cushion value in a way that could reorder two items by a learned transform of a regulated number (§8.5). Tickler classes: covenant next-test (keyed to `LLC_BI__Frequency__c` via last-eval + frequency), relationship reviews, collateral revaluations (`LLC_BI__Collateral_Valuation__c` freshness), UCC/insurance/flood expiry, and KYC refresh. KYC refresh respects empty-not-pass (§4.7): an unpopulated or unswept KYC object reads "unverified, blocks decisioning," never "due" or "clear." For Piedmont the queue concludes "DSC covenant next test (by `Frequency__c`) is the item to watch; the cushion is thinning toward it." Drills into the single-customer 360 of the relationship behind each tickler. (Full surface contract: `MAINTENANCE-AND-MONITORING.md`.)

### 5.6 The Authority / Routing model (gap 3)

*Blueprint stage 04, Approval. Extends §4.1 (header verdict), §4.6 (the verdict for the approver), §6.4 (Credit Officer), the editable-floor of the interaction model (§7.2), and §10.1/§10.2 (SR 11-7 + policy exceptions).*

**The question.** The Credit Officer walks in with two questions the credit verdict leaves implicit: **"Is this mine to approve, or does it go up?"** and **"If I approve it, am I inside my authority or am I the exception?"** The cockpit already concludes the credit (Grade 5 supported, coverage carries it, margin is the watch) and wires the approve/reject control to that verdict. The missing half of stage 04 is whether **this officer, acting now, has the authority to land that write at all.** Approval is two stacked decisions: *is the credit good*, and *am I the right level to say so*. This pillar makes the second a first-class, server-computed verdict, the same way exposure and covenant grade are.

**The conclusion, not the table.** The anti-pattern is a "Lending Authority" tab showing a matrix of grades against dollar limits and leaving the officer to find their own row. The pillar concludes instead, in one banker sentence in the header verdict:

> **For Piedmont, to the acting Credit Officer (delegated limit $10M, secured C&I):** "This package requires **$12.5M / Grade 5** approval authority. Your delegated limit is **$10M**. **This is above your authority. Route to committee.** You may sponsor it up, attach your recommendation, and record rationale, but the approve write is committee-only on this package."

> **For the same package, to a Senior Credit Officer (delegated limit $25M):** "Requires $12.5M / Grade 5 authority. Your limit is $25M. **Within your authority. Approvable.** One open item: the single-obligor hold-limit check is unconfigured in this tenant, so the hold-limit clearance reads 'pending policy config', not 'clear'."

Two officers, one package, two honest verdicts, the same deterministic inputs. The officer lands on **a routing state**, not a grid. The grid is the drill. ($10M is the illustrative scenario, not a claimed Piedmont fact.)

**The two terms, both deterministic, and what is computable today.** **Required approval level** is a server-computed function of the package, mirroring the `boomFinancials.js` discipline. Its first two inputs are computable today from verified fields: exposure magnitude (package `LLC_BI__TCE__c` = $12.5M, the rollup, never the $17.5M loan sum) and risk grade (`LLC_BI__Risk_Rating__c` = "5", a criticized grade raises the level independent of dollars). Its third input, the **exception-load term** (any open `policy-exceptions` exception elevates the required level by rule), depends on the same unconfigured policy thresholds (single-obligor hold limit, pricing grid) that §10.2 defers as "pending policy config," so the exception-elevation term is itself "pending policy config." The required level is therefore **only partially computable today**: exposure × grade yes, exception-load no. **Acting officer's delegated limit** is read from the running identity, not asserted: the verified package approver fields `Approver_1__c` / `Approver_2__c` (plus routed `LLC_BI__Primary_Officer__c` / `LLC_BI__Secondary_Officer__c`), mapped through the `approverFields` config knob (PERSONAS.md §4.4), resolve the officer-of-record, but the delegated *limit* itself is a **policy-config quantity, not a sandbox-verified field** (`lendingAuthorityModel`). The routing verdict is the deterministic comparison `requiredLevel(package) <= actingLimit(user)` → approve-within-authority, else route-to-committee; computed in the same server tier as the covenant grade, narrated by the LLM but never decided by it.

**Where it surfaces.** Authority context is first-class in the header/verdict (§4.1's routing chip, persona-resolved to the approve-capable persona) and in the Credit Officer flow (§6.4), not a buried tab. The approve/reject control is **gated by the routing state, server-side, ahead of the credit verdict**, in three states: within authority → control live, officer approves and records rationale; above authority → the approve write is **withheld at the service tier, not merely greyed** (a manipulated or read-induced `ncino_approve_package` call by an under-authority officer is rejected server-side), and the officer gets sponsor-up (attach recommendation, record rationale, route to committee); within authority but exception-bearing → approvable, but the open exception rides forward as a condition.

**Why it lives on the locked floor.** The required level, the acting limit, and the routing state are **sourced figures, locked for everyone** (an officer cannot reframe their own limit upward; an agent cannot recompute the state to unlock a write). Editable: the officer's recommendation when sponsoring up, the rationale, the narrative. God-mode is visibility, not authority (the §10.5 invariant holds exactly): View-All is not approval-all; a god-mode session still cannot approve above the acting principal's authority. This is the SR 11-7 seam: DRAFT-until-committee is not just "a human commits" but "the *authorized* human commits, or it escalates."

**The honesty rule (empty-not-pass, applied to authority).** Where `lendingAuthorityModel` is configured, the cockpit concludes a real routing verdict. Where it is **not** configured (as in this sandbox, consistent with §10.2 and open question §13.3), the cockpit concludes "exposure × grade computable; exception-load and acting authority unconfigured, routing pending policy config" and **withholds the approve control by default (fail-closed)**, the same discipline as unverified KYC blocking decisioning. We can state the *exposure × grade* required side ($12.5M / Grade 5) from verified fields today; the *exception-load* term, the *acting-limit* side, and the final verdict light up the moment the tenant supplies its delegated-authority and exception policy, with no code change.

**Config knobs (cross-referenced in the §5.7 enumeration).** `lendingAuthorityModel` (delegated limit per User/UserRole, policy-config, absent → routing pending), `singleObligorHoldLimit` and `pricingGrid` (the §10.2 unconfigured policy thresholds feeding the exception-load term), `approverFields` (the package approver-field mapping), `personaResolution` (the routing chip is a personal verdict only for the approve-capable persona). All are collected in the §5.7 "Config knobs (both pillars)" block.

### 5.7 Persona-scoped FLS plus partial-view (gap 7) — the visibility pillar

*Cross-cutting, every stage. Promotes to a named primitive the field-level scoping and partial-view honesty v1 distributed across §4.1, §4.4, §4.6, §10.4, §10.5.*

**Why a pillar.** The cockpit already does the right thing in pieces (the header partial-view marker, the entity-graph re-check, the Treasury NII exclusion, the drill stopping at a marker, run-as-user as the floor). Scattered across sections, scope reads as a per-section courtesy rather than a designed control. This pillar names the primitive once, defines its two halves, and makes every section a consumer rather than a re-implementer. The principle: **the cockpit shows you exactly what Salesforce sharing and FLS grant your running identity, concludes honestly about what it cannot show, and never lets an aggregate, a drill, or a learned ranking silently cross a boundary the platform would deny.**

**Half 1, field-level scoping (FLS): the row is visible, a field on it is not.** The canonical case is Treasury (§6.6): the Treasury officer sees the relationship, deposits, the treasury-service gap, and fee/deposit revenue, but is FLS-denied on net interest income (NII) and deep credit-economics fields. The rule is **FLS-before-aggregation**: a field the running identity cannot read is excluded **before** any total is computed, not blanked after. The Treasury officer's "relationship economics" total is a wallet-side total, never an NII-inclusive total with the NII silently subtracted; the number they see is one they are entitled to every component of. For Piedmont (profitability empty) the rule is latent; it becomes load-bearing on Timothy Norton Household ($910,800 populated) where the Treasury revenue lens and the Credit Officer NII-inclusive lens read the same records and arrive at different, each-fully-entitled totals. FLS scoping also governs the **PII fence**: name, role, ownership %, entity type, guaranty amount render; SSN / TIN / DOB / personal address **never render to any persona, even where FLS would permit** (a hard floor above platform FLS, GLBA-driven, on the PersonAccounts Margaret Holloway and the Timothy Norton principals).

**Half 2, the partial-view marker: N entities the platform will not let you see.** A relationship can include affiliates, guarantors, or household members the running identity is not shared on. The rule is **partial-view honesty**: every related entity is re-checked against sharing **at draw time and at every drill level**; entities the user cannot see are **omitted and counted**, and the section renders "partial view, N related entities not visible to you." A rollup over a partial set says so; a number is never silently short. For Piedmont the marker is **absent** (the 2-node graph is fully shared to standard credit personas), and that absence is itself a conclusion: the banker can trust the $12.5M rollup is whole. The marker earns its place on multi-entity cases (a sister affiliate in another officer's book, an unshared household member). The two halves compose: FLS scopes *within* a record, the marker scopes *across* records, and a relationship can be partial on both axes at once.

**Scope is a control, enforced.** Three properties, all server-side: (1) **platform-enforced floor, not re-implemented** (the cockpit runs as the user via per-user OAuth, the `sobject-sf` shape, so sharing and FLS are platform-native on every query; FLS-before-aggregation and the traversal re-check are the cockpit honoring what the platform enforces, never a parallel access system; the deliberate upgrade over the memo's service-account-with-audit-stamp model); (2) **the marker is a designed output, not an error state** ("partial view, N not visible" and "field FLS-restricted" are conclusions with the same weight as any other, the destination of a blocked drill, not broken UI); (3) **self-learning runs inside the floor** (§8.5 invariant: the learning loop observes, ranks, and re-frames only over records the running user is already entitled to see; a learned signal never surfaces a conclusion about an entity the user cannot access, never builds a peer baseline over records outside the floor, never lets ranking leak the existence of a hidden affiliate).

**The one intentional exception.** The gated admin **god-mode** (elevated / View-All context, §10.5): behind an explicit switch plus permission, restricted from real users, every session audited, **visually unmistakable** via a persistent elevated-context banner. Its boundary is the same as the authority pillar's: **god-mode widens visibility; it never widens write authority.** Under god-mode the partial-view marker may clear and FLS-denied fields may render, but you always know you are seeing them under elevation, and the audit row records the elevated identity and the View-All flag so a god-mode read is never mistaken for an ordinary one.

**Every section consumes it.** Header (§4.1, rollup reflects only shared entities, marker in the header when N > 0, absent for Piedmont); entity graph (§4.2, each node re-checked, PII fence on every node); deposits/treasury (§4.4, FLS-before-aggregation excludes NII before the wallet total); exposure and risk (§4.3, §4.6, rollups and counts over shared facilities only, Executive/PM book-rollup collapses to own book where `sharingModelGrantsHierarchyRollup` is false and says so); KYC (§4.7, the partial-view marker on related parties not visible, so an incomplete screen is never read as complete); drill (§7.1, every descent sharing-checked, the bottom of a blocked drill is the marker); self-learning and memory (§8, §9.3, ranking and recall obey the sharing floor). One primitive, every consumer, zero re-implementations.

**Config knobs (both pillars).** `lendingAuthorityModel` (delegated limit per User/UserRole, policy-config, absent → routing pending, §5.6); `singleObligorHoldLimit` and `pricingGrid` (the §10.2 unconfigured policy thresholds feeding the §5.6 exception-load term); `approverFields` (the §5.6 package approver-field mapping, `Approver_1__c` / `Approver_2__c` plus routed `LLC_BI__Primary_Officer__c` / `LLC_BI__Secondary_Officer__c`); `cockpitSectionVisibilityMap` / `writeToolSurfaceMap` (per-persona section + write scope, server-enforced, never just hidden); `sharingModelGrantsHierarchyRollup` (gates the Executive/PM book rollup; false/unknown → collapse to own book and say so); `treasuryOfficerField` = `Secondary_Officer__c` (the Treasury overlay's FLS profile); `personaResolution` (the routing chip is a personal verdict only for the approve-capable persona; a CCO resolves here, never to the read-only Executive); the god-mode permission/permission-set (the single audited bypass, open question §13.2). No knob and no learned signal can widen the floor or touch the routing state; only god-mode can widen visibility, visibly and audited, and never for write authority.

## 6. The personas

A persona is a question someone walks in with, a conclusion they are entitled to reach, and the action that unlocks. Two facts every persona inherits: sourced numbers are deterministic and provenance-locked for everyone (what differs is which conclusion the cockpit draws, not the numbers); and the book is exactly what Salesforce sharing grants the running identity (§5.7). Visibility is config (`cockpitSectionVisibilityMap`, `writeToolSurfaceMap`), the consequence of the question/conclusion/action design, enforced server-side, never just hidden. Each persona maps to the lifecycle stage(s) it owns.

**Grounded in real practice (full role reference: `ROLE-REQUIREMENTS.md`).** These personas map to how the jobs actually work in a US commercial bank, not abstractions. The RM is now measured nearly as much on deposit and treasury revenue as on loan production, which is exactly why the $0-wallet conclusion is the headline and not a buried tab. ## 7. Interaction model: drill-down plus editable

The cockpit is a verdict, not a viewer. The interaction model does two things: let a user fall through a conclusion to the evidence and source-of-source (drill-down), and let a user shape how the conclusion is framed and capture judgment (editable), while regulated numbers stay frozen by platform design. Drill-down walks down the provenance chain; editable operates only on the layer above the locked floor. Same wall, two sides.

### 7.1 Drill-down

Every headline is a door, and the door opens its own provenance. Three properties hold on every drill: the number never changes as you descend (the drill reveals the server-side computation already done, never recomputes client-side); the descent is sharing-checked at every level (a drill that would reach an unshared affiliate stops at a "partial view, N entities not visible" marker, §5.7); and the bottom of every drill is a provenance card naming the system of record, vendor (Boom / Snowflake / nCino), field API name, as-of date, and a click-through to the vendor app.

### 7.2 Editable: three layers and one locked floor

Exactly three editable layers and one locked floor; the boundary is enforced server-side, so a user or a manipulated agent who reaches for a locked value is rejected, not merely un-buttoned.

**The LENS** (how the conclusion is framed, freely editable, per-user per-session, changes presentation never the source): period (DSC and margin at any spread period, FY2023 vs current), ratio selection, dollars vs margin %, committed vs outstanding, wallet source (nCino vs FSC vs union, which is how the same relationship reads "$0" or populated), KYC origination-vs-refresh lens, authority routing-context vs personal-verdict. Logged as a view event; writes nothing to the system of record.

**The JUDGMENT** (the human's call, editable as first-class records never in-place): annotate (free-text, fenced as data); flag watch (the live Piedmont case, the flag sits beside the deterministic Compliant grade, never replacing it); analyst adjustment record (the load-bearing one, `{value, basis:'analyst-adjustment', rationale, by, asOf}`, covering add-backs and the pro-forma leverage, dual lineage if it touches a grade, second-person review if it moves a boundary, the source number never mutated); capture decision plus rationale (`record_decision` appends the WHY to the package ledger; per the SR 11-7 fence it informs future judgment but is never a source for a figure and never enters the examiner-facing record); the officer's sponsor-up recommendation when routing above authority (§5.6). Every judgment write is identity-bound to the authenticated session, append-only, with "agent proposed" entries distinct and non-agent-writable from "human confirmed".

**The NARRATIVE** (wholly the human's, fully editable text, DRAFT-until-committee): the analyst drafts and attests; the cockpit may pre-draft prose; section sign-off never promotes to a decision; separation of duties holds (analyst drafts = development, officer approves = validation via `ncino_approve_package`, the only authorized write transition, enforced in Salesforce against the acting user and the routing verdict).

**The LOCKED floor** (read-only for everyone, no exceptions, not the analyst, not the CCO, not god-mode which widens visibility not write authority): exposure roll-ups (TCE/TBE/TOE/Outstanding), covenant actuals and thresholds, the covenant grade and risk rating, collateral figures (lendable, advance rate, lien), guarantor figures (100% ownership, full-note guaranty, with PII below structure not returned at all), the authority routing state and its computable terms (§5.6), the KYC clearance state (§4.7), and Boom line items (the sourced current and prior-period values; the pro-forma leverage is a labeled adjustment on top, not part of the floor).

| Layer | Example on Piedmont | Editable? | What an edit does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lens** | DSC period; dollars vs margin %; wallet source (nCino vs FSC); KYC origination vs refresh | Yes (per-user, per-session) | Reframes presentation; recomputes nothing of record |
| **Judgment** | Flag DSC/margin as watch; EBITDA add-back and pro-forma leverage adjustment; `record_decision` rationale; sponsor-up recommendation | Yes (as layered records, never in-place) | Adds an attributed sibling record beside the source; dual-lineage if it touches a grade |
| **Narrative** | The credit write-up prose | Yes (authored, attested, DRAFT-until-committee) | Authors text; never a number |
| **Sourced figure** | $12.5M TCE; DSC 1.42x/1.25x; Grade 5; ~$10M lendable; 100% ownership; routing state; KYC clearance; Boom line items | No, locked for all | Rejected server-side; the only "change" is a layered adjustment record (judgment), never a mutation |

You may change how a number is framed, record what you conclude about it, and write the story around it. You may never change the number. The drill-down proves where every locked number came from; the editable layers add lens, judgment, and prose on top without reaching the floor. The same wall the examiner walks.

## 8. Self-learning

The cockpit gets sharper per relationship and across the book by tuning which conclusions surface, in what order, framed how. The one fence that governs everything: **learning moves ranking, surfacing, and framing; learning never moves a number.** The TCE is $12.5M because `LLC_BI__TCE__c` says so; no learning nudges it, and no learning touches the authority routing state or a KYC clearance.

**8.1 Signals (interactions and labels, never financial inputs).** Analyst adjustments to lens/judgment/narrative (captured as a before/after diff, e.g. a pro-forma-leverage note teaches the cockpit to surface that lens earlier on similar deals); decisions plus rationale (an approval conditioned on margin recovery teaches the causal link between the margin-watch flag and the condition); watch flags raised/cleared (hand-labelled supervised signal); view/lens telemetry (a PM who jumps to whitespace on every credit-only relationship teaches "lead with whitespace for credit-only"); approve/reject outcomes (the strongest label, kept distinct between development and validation); which NBAs were acted on vs ignored (a repeatedly-ignored NBA is down-ranked); covenant outcomes vs predictions (a validated watch raises the detector's confidence). None of these is ever a financial input.

**8.2 What improves (ranks/surfaces/frames, never computes).** Sharper conclusions (the header verdict evolves from a generic template to a tuned one; the grade is still read deterministically). Better-targeted NBAs (re-ranked, never re-valued; Piedmont's "win the wallet" stays number one because it converts on credit-only C&I; Timothy Norton's learned NBA is different in kind). Watch-item detection (the detector elevates which true fact becomes a watch card and at what confidence; the card's content, the numbers, is computed). Tickler ranking (§5.1, which due item leads, never the due date itself, and never a per-item urgency computed as a learned transform of the regulated cushion value, §8.5). Peer/segment baselines and institutional-memory recall (below).

**8.3 Peer / segment baselines, pinned and labeled.** The cockpit can frame Piedmont's 8.1% margin against a same-NAICS (332710) cohort. **This baseline is a NEW computed figure on a regulated surface, so it is governed exactly:** it is a deterministic server-side computation (same tier and discipline as `boomFinancials.js`, numbers never through the LLM), over records the running user is already entitled to see (§5.7); it renders with an explicit, visually distinct label "derived context statistic, NOT a sourced figure, not an input to this borrower's grade/covenant/exposure math" plus its own lineage chip (which cohort, N borrowers, as-of); it is descriptive/ranking-only, never enters the examiner-facing record, and never feeds `deal_covenant_grade`. Any specific cohort range (for example "~10 to 12%") is illustrative pending a real populated 332710 cohort, not asserted as sourced; Piedmont's own 8.1% / ~11% are the only asserted-real figures.

**8.4 The loop and where it is stored.** OBSERVE (capture an interaction event) to APPEND (write to the decision/learning ledger, provenance-stamped) to AGGREGATE (server-side batch, deterministic, the LLM never sums) to INFORM (next render re-ranks/re-surfaces/re-frames, never recomputes) to CLOSE (outcome labels the earlier conclusion). Asynchronous: observe and append live, aggregate in batch, apply at render time as a ranking layer over deterministic data. Stored in the decision/learning ledger (the `record_decision` / `recall_decisions` surface plus a distinct interaction-event class), per fleet SR 11-7 doctrine backed by the auditable Snowflake ledger; memory INFORMS, the ledger RECORDS, and memory is never a source for a regulated figure. Every learned conclusion is DRAFT, editable, a suggestion a human accepts/edits/rejects. Bank-agnostic: keyed on namespace plus config, bucketed per tenant on `NAICS_Code__c` / `Industry` / grouping key; a bank's learned signals never cross into another bank's cockpit.

**8.5 The SR 11-7 fence and the input invariant.** Allowed: re-order sections, promote a converting NBA, surface a watch card first, frame in a prior analyst's words, build a peer baseline for context, suggest a likely committee condition **as a prompt for human consideration** (never a predicted condition pre-populating an approval field or rationale). Forbidden: change any exposure roll-up, adjust a covenant grade or threshold, recompute a margin or leverage, originate a financial input from memory or behavior, feed a peer baseline into a borrower's own math, touch the authority routing state, alter a KYC clearance, make the credit decision. **The input invariant:** learning signals are read-only consumers of the deterministic outputs and may only affect ranking/surfacing/framing; no learned signal is ever an input to `deal_covenant_grade`, exposure roll-ups, collateral lendable math, the routing comparison, or any sourced field. This holds for tickler urgency too: the urgency score is a deterministic function of sourced inputs (days-to-test, cushion-percent, regulatory-clock), and learning may only re-weight *across* tickler classes, never compute a per-item urgency from a regulated cushion value in a way that could reorder two items by a learned transform of a regulated number. The only path to influence a grade is a human-authored adjustment record with second-person review, never a learned signal. Three guarantees backstop it: decision-support never autonomous; regulated numbers deterministic and provenance-locked; learning runs inside the sharing floor (as the running user, only records they can see; god-mode the single audited exception, visibility only).

The hundredth open of Piedmont leads with the same true facts the first had ($12.5M, $0 wallet, Grade 5, 8.1%, 1.42x), ordered and framed by everything the book taught the cockpit about relationships shaped like this one. The numbers are constant; the judgment about which of them matters most, to this persona, right now, is what gets smarter.

## 9. Memory: decision ledger plus audit plus recall

The cockpit remembers why the bank did what it did to Piedmont, not just what it shows today. When a different officer opens Piedmont next cycle, the cockpit greets them with "last cycle the team held the rating at 5 because EBITDA-margin compression was judged cyclical, not structural, re-test that thesis" before they read a number. Continuity of judgment across people and time, with the regulated numbers kept rigorously outside the memory.

**9.1 The WHY / WHAT split, two ledgers, never merged.**

The **decision ledger** (the WHY), written by `record_decision`: one entry per judgment, capturing the call ("held risk rating at 5"), the rationale ("margin compressed to 8.1% from ~11% but DSC still cleared at 1.42x and the compression traces to a one-time retooling, cyclical, hold the grade, flag margin as the watch"), the alternatives rejected ("considered downgrading to 6 on the margin trend, rejected because leverage at 3.85x is inside the Debt-to-Worth covenant and liquidity cushion is wide"), the who/when/role, and the confidence plus revisit trigger ("re-open if EBITDA margin prints below 7% or DSC cushion thins below 1.30x"). Editable institutional opinion, meant to be revised next cycle.

The **audit trail** (the WHAT), written by `log_audit_event`, read by `get_audit_trail`: one append-only row per consequential action, framed around the conclusion it enables, the officer's effective-challenge drill ("who attested what version, when, under which identity, so a downgrade reversal is never silent"). The event taxonomy (attestations, writes to the system of record, state transitions, routing/authority checks, god-mode entries) lives behind the drill; the trail leads with what it concludes for the reader (examiner-defensibility), not the list of row types. This is the substrate the Exam / QC Provenance Pack (§5.5) exports.

They cannot be one store: the ledger is mutable prose judgment (radioactive as evidence, perfect as a thinking aid); the trail is immutable structured identity-bound (perfect as evidence, useless for warming intuition). Separating them is what lets the cockpit be opinionated in the ledger and literal in the record an examiner reads.

**9.2 Persistence, tamper-evidence, identity binding.** The decision ledger persists per fleet SR 11-7 doctrine, backed by the auditable Snowflake decision ledger named in that doctrine (attributed there, not asserted as a verified Customer 360 implementation detail), keyed by the relationship anchor (Account `001bb00001DLtRMAA1`) and the package (`a5Fbb000000HA1NEAW`). The **published, examiner-facing memo artifact** is saved separately via `ncino_docman_save`. **The two stores are kept clean:** `record_decision` / `log_audit_event` is the live tamper-evident ledger (WHY/WHAT, decision-support context, NOT examiner-facing data); `ncino_docman_save` is the immutable published memo (the regulated record). Ledger rationale travels alongside the DocMan artifact as decision-support; it is never promoted into a DocMan field and never becomes examiner-facing data. Audit rows should be append-only and tamper-evident (a hash-chain is a recommended implementation, not an asserted property of the verified surface). The load-bearing, verified guarantee is that every entry is identity-bound to the authenticated Salesforce user from the run-as-user OAuth context (the `actorStamp()` discipline hardened, the actor is the authenticated principal, never an agent-supplied parameter); the gated admin god-mode records both the elevated identity and the View-All flag so a god-mode write is never mistaken for an ordinary one.

**9.3 `recall_decisions`, warming the next session.** On open, `recall_decisions(accountId)` runs first and primes the surface: a brand-new analyst sees the prior cycle's held-rating rationale, the rejected downgrade, the named watch item, and the revisit trigger. It surfaces drift, not just history, **and the drift conclusion is owned by the deterministic engine, not memory:** the live DSC cushion is re-read deterministically server-side, the proximity comparison against the stored revisit threshold (`watchThresholdPct` against today's sourced cushion) is the engine's output, and memory contributes only the previously-recorded threshold and rationale. The surfaced "the watch condition you set is now closer" is computed from today's sourced number, never recalled. Recall is cross-analyst by construction (keyed on the relationship, not the user) and obeys the sharing floor (an analyst only recalls decisions on relationships they can see, §5.7).

**9.4 How memory surfaces inline.** Not a separate history tab; threaded into the section it explains, as a conclusion plus a drill. On the risk section, beside the live Grade 5 and the four Compliant covenants: "Held at 5 last cycle, margin compression judged cyclical, thinnest cushion DSC 1.42x vs 1.25x [why drill]" (the grade itself is rendered from the deterministic engine, memory only annotates it). On the exposure/wallet headline: "Treasury cross-sell raised last cycle, deferred pending the credit decision, now unblocked." On Timothy Norton Household: "last review, share-of-wallet judged healthy at $910.8K, hold, do not push." On every transition control, the audit trail is one drill away.

**9.5 The SR 11-7 standing.** Memory informs judgment; it is never the source of a figure (Piedmont's TCE, grade, DSC, lendable collateral are re-derived live from the verified objects on every open, never read back from the ledger; if last cycle's ledger and today's math disagree, the math wins and the discrepancy is a flag). Memory never enters the examiner-facing regulated record as data (per 9.2). Separation of duties is preserved in the memory: the analyst's drafting attestations and the officer's approval attestations are distinct, attributed entries, and the memory makes the effective challenge durable (next cycle's analyst sees not just that the grade held, but that the officer pushed back on the downgrade case and why it was overruled).
