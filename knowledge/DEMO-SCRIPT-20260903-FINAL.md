## 1. Facility workroom, Hartwell, the $20M flow (3 min)
1. Open Hartwell, FAB, Facility. Hartwell now stages two packages, so the room asks first: pick the C&I package (six booked facilities, the $3M Proposal, "Non-Real Estate" first in the label). Then the greeting is route-neutral: book, parties, covenants, plan, the three routes; James's email is baked in with its route chip.
2. Click the $15M Line of Credit chip. One line: what should change on it.
3. `increase to 20M`. Card, confirm. The settled row collapses, the amortisation question glides up. Coverage-thins advisory is its own item: acknowledge it, the chips stay.
4. `240`, then `October 1, 2026`, or the chips. `Leave pricing for later` is always there.
5. Rate ask: chips `Hold 7.60% fixed, paid monthly`, `New all-in rate`, `Index + spread`. Type `Yes, 7.25% all-in`. The card shows 7.25%; the package total on the card is the staged $59M.
6. Composer +: $15M line, Legal Entity, remove: the real parties. Pick one, the line lands with the placeholder selected, edit, send.
7. `add a new 3M equipment loan with a 60 month term for CNC line expansion`, chips for amortisation and first payment. Then `add Elena Hartwell as limited guarantor on the new equipment loan`, `add a debt service coverage of borrower covenant of 1.30 on the new loan`, `add a 1% origination fee on the new equipment loan`. Each card sits under the $3MM Equipment on the rail.
8. Review and execute. Plan read-back: commitment first inside the $15M line, then rate, amortisation, first payment; the new facility with its three riders. Approve, execute: new package version with the clones and the new facility; "Purpose written" a few seconds later. Revert afterwards with the drive file's command (knowledge/MOD-NEW-LOAN-DRIVE-20260903.md).

## 2. Relationship room, Hartwell (2 min)
1. FAB, Relationship. Greeting with the governance signal, six routes as chips (`Add a covenant or an asset` is the sixth).
2. Covenant review: six covenants, Pending, next tests 2026-09-30 / 09-08 / 11-01. Each answered step leaves one row.
3. `add a Minimum Liquidity covenant of at least 2,000,000 tested quarterly from October 1`: type from the org's catalogue, operator and threshold in one question, frequency, effective date on the real clock, approve, execute. Lands on the account, zero loan junctions.
4. `add a warehouse at 1400 Industrial Parkway, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 valued at 3.2M as collateral`: type and sub-type, address, value, opening valuation. Zero pledges.
5. Collateral read: each asset with last valuation, method, next due or "overdue since". Ask for a field exam: one honest sentence, two chips.
