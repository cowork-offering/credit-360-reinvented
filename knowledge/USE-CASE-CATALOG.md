# Commercial Credit Brain — Credit 360 Cockpit: Persona & Use-Case Catalog

The cockpit is the CUSTOMER view, not the deal view. The customer is the obligor / relationship group (operating entity + owners/principals + guarantors + affiliates + subsidiaries + household), assembled from the nCino Connection graph + Legal Entities and enriched by the FSC Household. A single deal (one nCino Product Package) is one facet, a drill-in. Every use case rolls up at the group / obligor grain.

## The way of thinking

Every use case follows one shape:

- **Trigger** — the concrete event that brings the persona in (a tickler, a new spread, a covenant test, a utilization spike, a new beneficial owner, an OFAC hit, a maturity clock).
- **Question** — the exact banker sentence in their head.
- **Conclusion** — what the cockpit *concludes* for them: one banker-grade verdict at the customer/group level, never a table.
- **Action** — what it unlocks: drill to source, capture a decision, flag a watch, route for approval, stage a cross-sell, schedule a review.
- **Reads** — the real objects / tools behind the conclusion.

Two invariants throughout (SR 11-7): the cockpit **concludes and routes, a human commits**; every regulated number is **deterministic server-side, never LLM-computed**. Where a regulated figure is the persona's own judgment (a recovery estimate, a loss-content call, an add-back), it is captured as a proposed-not-committed decision, never produced by the model. Examples (Piedmont single-entity C&I; the FSC household) are labeled illustration, never the subject.

**A note on the shared illustration.** The Piedmont figures (DSC 1.42x vs 1.25x; EBITDA ~11%→8.1%; $12.5M committed / $4.25M drawn; ~$10M lendable; leverage 3.85x→~4.8x pro forma) recur across personas as a single worked example. They are *evidence anchors*, not the conclusion. Each persona's conclusion leads with its own verdict (RM: the relationship and wallet move; PM: the rating/watch call; CA: which line is soft and its lineage; CO: what to challenge and commit; Ops: what blocks funding; TM: the wallet; BSA: who is cleared; SA: the recovery posture). The ratios are the same; the verdict is not.

**A note on the sandbox data.** The seeded org is shallow: all Connection rows carry the generic "Connection" role rather than the 35-role taxonomy, so the live differentiating signal is ownership percentage, not role labels. Use cases that lean on rich roles (RM-5, PM-5, CA-3, BSA-2, BSA-3, SA-6) are demonstrated on ownership-% per the model doc; role labels are a production enrichment, not a demo dependency.

## 4. Senior / Chief Credit Officer (Credit Officer / Approver)

Validation-side decision authority. Performs effective challenge on a drafted, attested package; holds approve / approve-with-conditions / modify / decline up to a delegated limit, routing above it to committee. ## Servicing lifecycle deepening — annual review, renewals, modifications

The 57 use cases above cover origination, approval, and day-to-day monitoring densely, but the *servicing lifecycle* — the recurring re-underwrite of an existing relationship (annual/periodic review), the formal **renewal** of a maturing facility (`LLC_BI__LoanRenewal__c`: ParentLoanId, RevisionNumber, Status, PreviousVersionStage/Status, HasActiveRenewalLoan; child `LLC_BI__Credit_Memo_Modifcation__c`), and the in-life **modification** of an existing note (`LLC_BI__Loan_Modification__c`: Mod_Type Notification|Approval, Modification_Type ∈ {Increase/Reduce Loan Amount, Interest Rate Reduction, Payment Deferral, Extend Amortization, Interest Only, Correct Existing Note}, Owner ∈ {Credit, Servicing, Loan Ops, Compliance, Finance}, Status New→Under Review→Approved/Rejected/Recalled, New_Amount, New_Rate, Effective_Date, linked `LLC_BI__Loan__c`) — was thin. These 14 deepen it across the personas that own the post-close relationship. Same two SR 11-7 invariants hold: **the cockpit concludes and routes, a human commits**; every regulated figure (exposure rollup, covenant grade, lendable, re-rate inputs) is **deterministic server-side, never LLM-computed**, with a modification's New_Amount/New_Rate/Effective_Date treated as proposed-not-committed until the Status crosses to Approved. The Piedmont single-entity C&I illustration recurs as the worked example.

## Cross-cut B: top 10 highest-value use cases

Ranked by leverage: how often it fires, how much money/risk it touches at the group grain, and how hard the conclusion is for a banker to reach unaided today.

1. **PM-1 — Morning book-health triage (which name rises today).** The deterministic urgency score across ~80 names, daily. The single most-used surface in the cockpit; it is the front door to almost every other Servicing use case.
2. **RM-1 — Walk into the annual review already prepared on the whole group.** One banker sentence that re-defends an entire $12.5M relationship; replaces the days-long memo scramble and anchors the highest-stakes recurring conversation.
3. **CO-1 — Effective challenge on a package in the approval queue.** The SR 11-7 spine made operational: the conclusion tells the officer exactly what to push on, with lineage, at the moment of commit.
4. **CO-4 / BSA-1 — KYC clearance gate that cannot be approved through.** The deterministic server-side block is the cockpit's strongest "decision-support refuses to enable an unsafe action" proof point, and it spans Sales, Approval, and account opening.
5. **CA-2 — The one true group exposure number ($12.5M, not $17.5M).** Every downstream conclusion (coverage, concentration, forbearance, boarding) depends on trusting the netted rollup over the facility sum. Foundational.
6. **RM-2 / SA-1 — Catch the slide in-quarter, before the test.** Margin-as-the-lever-under-DSC, surfaced a quarter early. This is the difference between a managed conversation and a workout; it converts a calendar-driven bank into an event-driven one.
7. **RM-3 / TM-1 — Win the operating wallet on a credit-rich, deposit-poor group.** $12.5M of risk, $0 of wallet: the single largest unmonetized opportunity pattern in the book, with the work split clean (RM ranks and hands off; Treasury sizes and converts).
8. **CO-3 / PM-5 / PM-7 — Concentration, per-decision and book-wide.** PM-7's standing book-level dashboard and CO-3's concentration-at-decision turn approval into a portfolio-aware act, catching the single-key-person concentration the single-entity view hides, against Tier 1 + ACL.
9. **CO-6 — Exam-ready effective-challenge defense.** The append-only Boom→Snowflake→nCino trail with AI-proposed vs human-attested separation. The artifact that survives an examiner; the reason the whole governance posture is credible.
10. **Ops-1 — Boarding reconcile to the netted rollup before the funding gate.** The last deterministic check before money moves; catches the $17.5M-vs-$12.5M over-commitment exactly where it would otherwise become a live over-advance.
